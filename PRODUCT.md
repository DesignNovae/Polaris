# Polaris

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students planning academic goals and using learning and exam tools. Administrators manage platform content and notices.

## Product Purpose

Connect academic planning, learning, evidence, and daily work in one workspace.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing application uses Next.js, Clerk identity, and MongoDB application data. The notice feature supports drafts, publishing, archiving, and administrator-selected audiences. The notification bell opens a floating paper over the current page, with private unread state. There is no separate user-facing notice workspace. Actual publication requires an authenticated administrator. The paper keeps the supplied dark Site of the Year design with brown accents. Loading uses a large transparent Polaris orb across the application.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the Polaris name, compass mark, existing light and dark themes, and workspace navigation. The user requested ThreeUI's Glassmorphism CTA buttons, Brand Orbs adapted to Polaris, and Site of the Year paper motion for notices. Preserve supplied source provenance while adapting content and integration.

## Product Principles

- Connect loading feedback to real pending work.
- Preserve readable content and usable controls when animation is unavailable.
- Enforce administrative permissions and notice audiences on the server.
- Verify functional behavior and responsive layouts before reporting completion.
